# Frozen Current Scientific Conclusion

**Frozen before Phase 2D:** 2026-11-01

The project does not support a universal non-commutative Shift Algebra. It supports the narrower proposition that target-free composition measurements can improve forecasting of frozen-model behavior, and that development-informed pair selection can reduce calibration cost relative to random pair measurement.

Frozen result interpretation:

1. Phase 1 and Phase 1B reject the original long-word algebra under independent protocols.
2. Phase 2 supports incremental value from full composition forecasts beyond source/model/word metadata.
3. Phase 2B rejects ACHM as a superior replacement for the prior `g1`, despite a small point-MAE gain over fixed hazards.
4. Phase 2C supports one five-pair selection heuristic over expected random five-pair calibration, but not global optimality or full-budget equivalence.
5. Order and expert-disagreement uncertainty are not supported as headline claims.

Phase 2D will not revise any method. It applies already frozen `g0`, `g1`, full ACHM, active/random five-pair designs, and development-frozen alternative selectors to two architecture families absent from all method development. A positive or negative result will be retained. No Phase-2D outcome can retroactively change the gates above.

## Recorded Phase-2D outcome

The formal architecture-holdout gate failed because the six-lineage clustered interval included zero, although `g1` improved point MAE, controlled rank, and regret. The earlier active-pair gate also did not fully replicate. A post-outcome audit found no leak, but confirmed heterogeneity (five of six positive lineages; exact lineage sign-flip p=0.0625 one-sided) and only two family-level units. The four-bank synthesis is retrospective and not independent-dataset confirmation. These results narrow rather than replace the frozen conclusion.

## Recorded Phase-2E outcome

A second prospectively frozen breadth extension added four new families and twelve lineages. `g1` reduced MAE from 4.443pp to 2.034pp and improved rank and regret, but TinyAttention was negative and the family-hierarchical interval [-0.072, 5.287]pp included zero. The all-required gate failed 4/5 criteria. Across five frozen banks, `g1` improves every bank's aggregate MAE, but all banks still share CIFAR-10 and related transformations; the retrospective synthesis is not independent-dataset confirmation. Across the six prospective holdout families, five favor `g1`; the independence-assuming family sign test is one-sided p=0.1094, so family breadth remains unconfirmed.

## Recorded Phase-2F outcome

Phase 2F restored the complete six-coordinate behavior output on canonical CIFAR-100 with separate development and sealed confirmation banks. Composition-aware full-pair forecasting reduced macro normalized MAE from 0.554 to 0.254, improved all six coordinates, produced hierarchical improvement CI [0.152, 0.538], raised macro controlled rank from 0.302 to 0.811, and lowered accuracy regret. The complete 5/5 gate passed. Prospectively selected magnitude five-pair calibration also passed against expected random selection (0.271 versus 0.338 macro normalized MAE; improvement CI [0.024, 0.133]).

This supports transfer of the forecasting methodology to CIFAR-100 and a full behavior vector. It is not strict coefficient transfer: metric-specific coefficients and exponents were developed on the CIFAR-100 development split before sealed confirmation. It does not revive the universal Shift Algebra, order, uncertainty, natural-shift, or modern-model claims.
